User Manual
How to export Roznamchah Sabaq to Excel?
Navigate to Records & Studio > Comprehensive Reports, select your Nazm/Daurah class, and tap "Export Daily Sabaq (Excel Format)" to generate the monthly attendance & hifz log.
Printing Student ID Cards in batch
Open Certificates & ID Studio from the side drawer. Choose the current academic term (1448 AH) and click "Print Sheet (PVC 8-Up)".
Configuring Azan & Jamat prayer timings
Access Madrasa Settings > Prayer Clock Synchronization to adjust geographical offsets for Guntur district coordinates.
Resetting teacher or guardian passwords
Super-Admin/Nazim can initiate one-click SMS OTP resets from Staff Management or send direct credentials via WhatsApp Gateway.
